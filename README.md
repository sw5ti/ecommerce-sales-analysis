# Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales, profit, quantity, orders, and profit margin.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance and allows users to explore sales performance across different years, regions, customer segments, categories, subcategories, and states.

## Objectives
The main objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales performance and provide an interactive dashboard that can help identify sales trends, profitable categories, high-performing subcategories, and regional performance.

## Business Questions
The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

- What are the overall sales, profit, quantity sold, and number of orders?
- How do sales and profit change month by month?
- Which product categories contribute the most to profit?
- What percentage of total sales comes from each category?
- Which states generate higher sales?
- Which subcategories have the highest sales?
- How does performance vary across different regions?
- How does performance differ across customer segments?
- How does business performance change across different years?
- What is the overall profit margin?

## Dashboard Features
The dashboard includes:

- Sales KPI
- Profit KPI
- Quantity KPI
- Number of Orders KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Year-over-Year growth indicators
- Monthly Sales and Profit Analysis
- Category-wise Profit Analysis
- Category-wise Sales Share
- State-wise Sales Map
- Top 5 Subcategories by Sales
- Interactive Year, Region, and Segment filters

## Key Insights
- Phones and Chairs are among the highest-sales subcategories in the displayed analysis.
- Technology records the highest category-wise profit in the displayed dashboard.
- Category-wise sales are distributed across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Monthly sales and profit show variation throughout the year, with stronger performance visible during several later months.
- Sales performance varies across states, allowing high- and low-performing locations to be identified.
- The dashboard allows performance to be compared across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office customer segments.
- Year and regional filters allow users to explore changes in business performance across different periods and locations.
